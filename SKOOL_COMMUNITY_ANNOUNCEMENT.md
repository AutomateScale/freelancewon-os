**🎁 Free for the community: FreelanceWon OS, the Upwork system we use ourselves**

Most freelancers don't lose on Upwork because of skill. They lose because their proposals sound like everyone else's and their pipeline lives in their head.

So we packaged the system we run on our own Upwork bids and made it **free and open source** for everyone here.

**What you get**

🛡️ **Upwork Jobs, with your own login.** Connect your Upwork account through Upwork's official MCP. Load Upwork's own Best Matches, search, or paste any job link. No scraping, no bots, no shared accounts.

✍️ **Our proposal playbook.** Pick a **Full proposal** (one sharp question about their setup, 3 to 6 short lines, your proof) or a **Quick note** (1 to 3 sentences that sound like you typed them between calls). Draft it with your own AI key, or write it yourself.

✅ **Checks before anything is sent.** Contact details are blocked (that's an Upwork rule), and em dashes, prices in the letter and "Dear Hiring Manager" openers are flagged.

👀 **You approve every proposal.** Upwork shows you the preview and the Connects cost. Nothing goes out until you click Submit.

📊 **Pipeline CRM, pricing calculator and SOW builder.** Track every lead from proposal to retainer.

🔔 **Coming soon: WhatsApp job alerts.** Save a search and get new jobs on your phone. It's built; we're switching it on once it's confirmed with Upwork.

**Get it (about 5 minutes)**

1. Download it: https://github.com/AutomateScale/freelancewon-os
2. Run `npm install`, copy `.env.example` to `.env` and add your Anthropic or OpenAI API key
3. Run `npm start`, open http://127.0.0.1:8787, then go to **Upwork Jobs → Connect Upwork**

Everything runs on your own computer. Your Upwork login and your data stay with you.

Then make the playbook yours: edit `playbook/proposal-playbook.md` so the drafts sound like you.

Comment **"FW OS"** with what kind of work you bid on and I'll help you tune your playbook. ⚡
