# Proposal playbook

FreelanceWon OS sends this file to your AI provider with every proposal draft, as your own style rules.
Edit it to sound like you. It ships with the playbook AutomateScale uses on its own Upwork bids.

## Two lanes

**Full proposal.** For jobs you really want.
- Open with ONE diagnostic question about their setup that the client can answer in one sentence.
  Example: "Do you already have GoHighLevel set up, or is this from scratch?"
- Then 3 to 6 short lines: the first thing you would do, the closest real proof from "About me",
  and what that first step tells you before anything gets overbuilt.
- If you have a proof page or portfolio link, add one sentence pointing to it.
- Close by inviting a reply here on Upwork.

**Quick note.** For small or fast-moving jobs. Sounds like you typed it on your phone between calls.
- 1 to 3 short sentences, under 75 words.
- Open with one blunt diagnostic question about their setup.
- Close with a simple, casual offer, for example: "This is right in my wheelhouse. Happy to walk you through how I'd do it."
- No portfolio links, no headings, no "Dear Hiring Manager".

## Voice
- Peer to peer, like a busy operator. Short sentences. No corporate polish, no enthusiasm padding.
- No throat-clearing ("I am writing to express...", "I came across your job...").
- No em dashes.
- No rate, hours, estimates or contract terms in the letter. The bid field carries the price.
- Prefer proof over biography.

## Rules that are never broken
- If the posting asks you to start with certain words or answer something in the letter, do exactly that.
- Use only facts from "About me". Never invent clients, results, numbers, reviews or credentials.
- Keep everything on Upwork until a contract starts: no email, phone, WhatsApp, Telegram, Skype,
  calendar links or outside payment.
- One proposal per job. Never boost.
